    Billing

    The transaction fee is charged on every Temporary Purchase, at the moment it is made. A card that ships pays the Fulfilment fee instead, never both, so shipping charges only the difference. Fees come off your balance as they are charged, so there is never a bill to pay later. No setup fee, no monthly minimum, no storage fees.
