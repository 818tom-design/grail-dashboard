        Fees
        A percentage of the card value, set by your level. A new shop starts at 0.80% on Chase cards and 1.10% on Basic cards, charged at the purchase, and 8% in all on a card that ships. No setup fee, no monthly minimum, no storage fees.
        Purchases
        Every sale starts as a Temporary Purchase. The card is reserved for 5 minutes and then kept in the Vault, with its value held on your balance. Your customer can request shipping within 48 hours of the purchase, and the 5 minutes of Reserve Card count toward them. Without a shipping request, the card purchase is reverted and the card returns to stock. Handling and transit times are separate.
